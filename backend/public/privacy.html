AI Ready Tools Extension

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The AI Ready Tools Extension adds a sidebar of saved prompts and skills to claude.ai. Clicking one types it into Claude's chat box for you to review; the extension never sends a message on your behalf. This page explains exactly what data the extension handles.

If you never sign in

Your buttons, tabs, and settings are stored only in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. The usage rings shown on claude.ai are read from your own claude.ai account and displayed locally; they are not sent anywhere while you are signed out.

If you sign in to use an organisation

Signing in is optional and only needed for shared team prompts. When you sign in and are a member of an organisation, the following is stored on our servers:

DataWhyWho can see it
Your email addressTo identify you and find or create your organisationYour organisation's admins
Your organisation membership (role, status, who invited you)To show the right shared prompts and admin toolsYour organisation's admins
Your claude.ai usage percentages (session, weekly, extra usage)So admins can see whether the team is near its usage limitsYour organisation's admins
How many times you have sent each shared prompt, and when you last didSo admins can see which shared prompts are usefulYour organisation's admins
Shared prompts and tabs created by adminsSo every member gets the same team promptsEveryone in that organisation
Organisation-wide daily run totals (no individual breakdown)Team activity overviewYour organisation's admins

Usage percentages are reported only while you are signed in and an active member; reporting stops when you sign out or leave. If an admin invites you, nothing is shared until you accept the invite.

What we never collect

  • The content of your Claude conversations, or what you type into Claude.
  • Your personal buttons and prompts (they stay in your browser).
  • Your browsing history or activity on any site other than claude.ai.

Service providers

We use these companies to run the service. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Clerk: sign-in. Clerk receives your email address and basic profile from the sign-in method you choose (for example Google or email).
  • Vercel: hosts our server.
  • Neon: hosts our database.

To protect the service from abuse, our server keeps short-lived request counters keyed by IP address or account; these are deleted after one day.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the providers above and your own organisation's admins as described.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Leaving an organisation (Settings → Leave organisation), or being removed by an admin, deletes your membership, your reported usage, and your per-prompt run counts for that organisation.
  • Signing out stops all reporting. Your sign-in token is stored in your browser and expires after 14 days.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything stored in your browser.
  • To delete your account or an entire organisation, contact us at the address below.

Permissions the extension asks for

  • Access to claude.ai: to type your prompts into the chat box and show your usage.
  • Storage: to keep your buttons and settings in your browser.
  • Side panel: to show the sidebar.
  • Identity: to open the optional sign-in window.
  • Our server and sign-in provider: used only when you sign in.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we will update this page and its date before the change ships.
